feat: Ambulance Tracking, Zone Automation & UI Refactor

This PR introduces a comprehensive overhaul of the EMS Ambulance Tracking and Assignment system.

Key Features:
- Live Tracking & Map Enhancements: Real-time movement tracking, status inference, and zone visualization.
- Zone-Based Automation: Automatic zone logging, retroactive assignment, and trip sequence detection.
- UI/UX Improvements: Refactored Assignment Card with modular components (Timeline, Timer, Grid) and a smart assignment modal with split view and Saudi timezone support.
- Technical & Cleanup: Extracted reusable components and removed unused backend scripts.
